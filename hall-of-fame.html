Lamad Squad Fantasy Football League

Hall of Fame

The LSFFL Hall of Fame recognizes retired franchises whose sustained excellence, championships, innovation, and lasting influence helped shape the Lamad Squad Fantasy Football League. Induction honors organizations that defined an era, inspired future competitors, and left a legacy that continues to be part of LSFFL history.

The Golden Era of the LSFFL (2002–2010)

The years from 2002 through 2010 marked one of the most dominant periods in LSFFL history. During that span, Bears and Geishas established themselves as the league's premier franchises, creating a rivalry defined by consistent excellence and championship-caliber football. Together they captured three LSFFL Championships, nine division titles, five scoring championships, and five regular-season titles, while making deep playoff runs year after year. Their sustained success set the competitive standard for the league and helped establish the tradition of excellence that continues to define the LSFFL today.
